# Microsoft MB-240 Study Guide

## Introduction

The **MB-240: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Field Service Functional Consultant** exam was designed for functional consultants implementing and configuring Dynamics 365 Field Service solutions.

**Important status:** Microsoft retired MB-240 and the related **Microsoft Certified: Dynamics 365 Field Service Functional Consultant Associate** certification on **June 30, 2026**. This repository is intended for historical reference, learning Dynamics 365 Field Service concepts, and understanding the former exam objectives. It is not a guide to booking a currently available MB-240 exam.

---

Microsoft's final published skills measured the ability to configure Field Service, manage work orders and customer assets, schedule and dispatch, manage the mobile application, handle inventory and purchasing, and implement Power Platform.

---

## Who Should Take It?

The former exam was designed for professionals who worked with:

- Dynamics 365 Field Service implementations
- Field service processes and work orders
- Resource scheduling and dispatch
- Customer assets and inspections
- Mobile field technicians
- Inventory and purchasing
- Power Platform customization and automation

Candidates were expected to understand how Field Service integrates with Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Microsoft 365, Power Platform, Remote Assist, and Guides.

---

## Exam Objectives / Domains

The final published objectives were:

1. **Configure field service applications — 20–25%**
2. **Manage work orders and customer assets — 25–30%**
3. **Schedule and dispatch work orders — 15–20%**
4. **Manage the Field Service mobile app — 5–10%**
5. **Manage inventory and purchasing — 5–10%**
6. **Implement Microsoft Power Platform — 5–10%**

These percentages and objectives reflect Microsoft's final study guide for MB-240.

## Detailed Study Notes

### 1. Configure Field Service Applications

Study:

- Territories, postal codes, organizational units
- Characteristics and proficiency models
- Resource roles and categories
- Field Service settings
- Trades and trade coverages
- Products, services, pricing rules, and price lists
- Bookable resources and frontline workers
- Working hours, time off, pay types, and rates
- Facilities and equipment
- Requirement groups, pools, and crews
- Remote Assist and Dynamics 365 Guides

Understand how configuration choices affect resource availability and scheduling.

### 2. Manage Work Orders and Customer Assets

Focus on the complete work-order lifecycle:

**Create → Schedule → Dispatch → Perform Work → Complete → Resolve**

Learn:

- Work order types
- Booking status and sub-status
- Incident types
- Service tasks
- Products and services
- Agreements and automatic work-order generation
- Entitlements
- Inspections
- Customer assets and child assets
- Functional locations
- Asset properties
- Work-order resolutions

### 3. Schedule and Dispatch

Understand:

- Schedule Board
- Schedule Assistant
- Quick Book
- Fulfillment preferences
- Resource requirements
- Requirement groups
- Filtering constraints
- Universal Resource Scheduling
- Geocoding
- Booking timestamps and journals

Scheduling should consider skills, location, availability, and resource requirements.

### 4. Field Service Mobile

Learn how technicians use the mobile application to:

- View and complete work orders
- Update booking status
- Complete service tasks
- Perform inspections
- Capture images and signatures
- Track time
- Work offline
- Maintain customer assets
- Use Remote Assist and Guides
- Use location tracking and geofencing
- Synchronize offline data

### 5. Inventory and Purchasing

Understand:

- Warehouses
- Product inventory
- Inventory adjustments
- Warehouse transfers
- Inventory journals
- Purchase orders
- Receivable products
- Product returns
- Return merchandise authorizations
- Return-to-vendor processes

### 6. Microsoft Power Platform

Study:

- Model-driven apps
- Forms
- Views
- Navigation
- Custom pages
- Canvas apps
- Power Automate cloud flows
- Dataverse-based customization

Focus on choosing the appropriate Power Platform capability for a Field Service requirement.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing work orders with bookings
- Ignoring resource characteristics and availability
- Misunderstanding incident types
- Skipping customer-asset relationships
- Treating the Schedule Board and Schedule Assistant as identical
- Forgetting mobile offline requirements
- Overlooking inventory transactions
- Using Power Platform components without understanding Dataverse relationships
- Studying outdated features without checking current Microsoft documentation
